The short version: **this site does not set any cookies of its own**, and we don't use analytics,
advertising, or social media tracking. That's why you don't see a cookie banner here — under the
GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive, a banner is only required when a site uses cookies that aren't
strictly necessary for it to function, and this one doesn't.

## What this site doesn't do

- No analytics (no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Umami — nothing).
- No advertising or retargeting pixels.
- No social media embeds or share buttons that track you.
- No cookie-based session for the qualification or partnership forms: submitting them doesn't
  set anything in your browser.

## What might still happen at the infrastructure level

Our hosting provider, **Netlify**, serves this site's files and processes form submissions.
Delivering a website at all involves standard web server logs (your IP address, browser type,
the page requested) for security and performance — this is true of any website on the internet,
Netlify's own included, and is not something this policy controls. It is not used to track you
across visits or across other sites.

If that ever changes — if we add analytics, a booking tool, or anything else that sets a
non-essential cookie — this policy will be updated first, and a consent banner will appear before
the change goes live.

## Your browser settings

You can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time; since this site doesn't rely on any,
doing so won't affect how it works for you.

See our [privacy policy](/en/privacy/) for how we handle the data you actively give us through
the forms on this site.
