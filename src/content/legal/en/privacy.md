## What we collect, and why

We only ask for what changes the advice we give you on the call. We do not collect financial
details, passport numbers, or any document scans through this website.

**If you fill in the qualification form (`/book/`):** your name, email, citizenship, intended
level and field of study, target intake year, where you are in the process, and — depending on
your citizenship — either your awareness of the consular financial requirement and whether you
want housing support, or your Erasmus stay length and housing search window. You may also tell us
which level of support interests you and add free-text notes.

We ask for your **citizenship**, not your nationality on a passport or your ethnicity: it is the
single fact that determines which visa and residence rules apply to you, and therefore which
questions the rest of the form asks. We ask whether you are **aware of the consular financial
requirement** (around €7,000/year) rather than for your family's income or savings, because
what we need to know is where you stand, not what you have.

**If you fill in the partnership form (`/partners/`):** your name, role, agency name, country,
work email, the volume of students you send abroad each year (as a bracket, not an exact figure),
the destinations you currently serve, what you are looking for, and any notes you add.

**In both cases:** whether you agreed to be contacted about Kimere's services beyond this specific
enquiry (marketing consent) — a separate, optional checkbox that has no effect on the call itself.

## Legal basis

We process this data on the basis of your **consent** (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the
consent checkbox before submitting either form. Marketing communications rest on a second, separate
consent, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting the qualification call.

## Who else sees this data

Form submissions are stored by **Netlify, Inc.**, our hosting provider, which processes them as our
data processor under Netlify's Data Processing Addendum and the Standard Contractual Clauses for
transfers outside the European Economic Area — Netlify's infrastructure is based in the United
States. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it to train
any model.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or social media tracking on this site, so no other third
party receives your data through it.

## How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for **24 months** from the date you send them, after which they are
deleted from our systems. If we start working together, the retention period for that relationship
is governed by the engagement itself, not by this website.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask us to:

- **access** the data we hold about you;
- **correct** it, if it's wrong or out of date;
- **delete** it, subject to any legal reason we might have to keep it;
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it;
- receive it in a **portable** format.

To exercise any of these, write to the certified email address in the section above once it is
confirmed. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the
[Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/), or with the
supervisory authority in your own country.

## Automated decisions

Nobody's application is accepted, rejected, or scored by an algorithm here. A person reads your
form and calls you.
