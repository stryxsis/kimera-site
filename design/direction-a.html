02 — The scale

The part after admission is a maze

Between your acceptance letter and your first day of class, you'll deal with a consulate, a university, a post office, an immigration office, a tax agency, and your local municipality — each with its own deadlines and its own paperwork.

  • 8 working days to file your residence permit after landing
  • 6+ different offices involved before you're fully settled
  • 90+ Italian universities, each with its own admission calendar

03 — How far we come with you

Three levels of support

Choose how far you want us to go with you — from getting your application right, to being there when you land, to having a verified place to live.

Level 01

Admission

Get the academic side right, from course choice to your visa file.

  • Course and university guidance matched to your background and budget
  • Universitaly pre-registration, tracked against each university's deadline
  • Qualification recognition — Declaration of Value or CIMEA, whichever applies to you

Level 02

Arrival

Everything in Admission, plus the first weeks on the ground — the part with the strict deadlines.

  • Everything in Admission
